Over the last year I've put a lot of thought into what I want the Artemis logo to communicate. This design incorporates a number of different elements:
 - the package/envelope communicates the essential message-centric nature of the broker
 - the arrow serves a number of different purposes
   - it incorporates the "goddess of the hunt" idea from the mythological Artemis
   - the subtle up-and-to-the-right direction of the arrow gives a nod to progress
 - the isometric design is simple but distinctive
 - it is a conscious move away from the previous logo using dots/nodes

![logo](ArtemisLogoAttempt1.svg)

I intentionally did not add any colors. However, the design lends itself to a tri-color theme (i.e. one color on each visible face) or perhaps just a unique color for the arrow. Here's a couple of **examples**:

![logo-2colors](ArtemisLogoAttempt1-2colors.svg)
